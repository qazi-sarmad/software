## Mapping notes for the catalogue

- **14.6 Engagement Documentation** ↔ workpapers, evidence, the Audit File, sealed artifacts.
- **14.4 Recommendations and Action Plans** ↔ findings, observations, CAPs.
- **15.1 Final Engagement Communication** ↔ the Writing Engine, report freeze and issue.
- **15.2 Confirming the Implementation of Recommendations or Action Plans** ↔ CAP follow-up and
  closure verification.
- **11.4 Errors and Omissions** ↔ pre-flight checks and the acknowledge-with-reason path.
- **9.3 Methodologies** ↔ the Phase 4 methodology / test-step templates.
- **5.1 / 5.2 Use and Protection of Information** ↔ Law L2, the RLS model, `jwt_org_id()`.
- **12.3 Oversee and Improve Engagement Performance** ↔ review, sign-off roles, approval chain.
